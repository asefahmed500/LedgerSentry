LedgerSentry. One agent, two jobs: reconcile payments against invoices, and check every contract clause against the rulebook.

Finance teams lose money on the messy twenty percent: partial payments, misspelled vendors, scanned invoices. Rules go quiet — the agent reasons through it.

Watch it work. This invoice, the strict baseline says unmatched. The agent says matched — ninety-five percent confidence — and explains why: exact amount, date in window, vendor resolved by fuzzy score.

Uploading is three steps: pick the scan, OCR fills the fields, link the purchase order it bills against. Every run is fully logged — every tool call, input, and output is the audit trail.

Compliance cites the exact clause behind every violation. And when the agent is unsure, it doesn't guess — it routes to human review with its reasoning attached.

One command scores baseline versus agent on the same labeled data: accuracy, false positives, partial handling, precision, recall. Measured, not vibes. The agent's most valuable output isn't the match — it's a reasoned I'm not sure.
